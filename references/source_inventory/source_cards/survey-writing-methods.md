## Summary

There is no single official cross-domain rulebook for human survey writing. A defensible survey-writing prior has to combine reporting/conduct guidance for scoping and evidence synthesis, narrative synthesis methods, taxonomy-construction methods, and venue-specific review article expectations.

## Decision Boundary

Survey-writing prior is not an automatic survey-generation pipeline. Reporting checklists do not replace thinking about scope, novelty, taxonomy, comparison dimensions, exemplar papers, and target venue conventions.

## Supported Uses

- Choose scoping, narrative, meta-narrative, realist, qualitative, or synthesis-without-meta-analysis guidance when the survey mode is explicit.
- Use taxonomy-method sources to structure paper families and concept hierarchies.
- Use venue guidance only for style, audience, length, novelty, and article-type expectations.
