## Operational rules

- Use this card for Scientific Reviews article-fit, manuscript-structure, and submission-package checks.
- Treat it as a venue/publisher policy source, not a method authority.
- Verify current live instructions before giving final word-count, display-item, file-format, or submission-system advice.
- Cite the canonical Markdown file or source URL for any venue claim; do not cite this derivative card as evidence.

## Common misuses

- Using Scientific Reviews guidance to define narrative reviews across fields.
- Treating the checklist as a quality-appraisal or reporting guideline.
- Applying Nature Reviews or Nature Portfolio policies interchangeably without journal-specific verification.
- Using the card to justify PRISMA, Cochrane, JBI, GRADE, or risk-of-bias claims.

## Evidence limits

The local evidence is venue guidance and a linked submission checklist. It supports local routing and preliminary writing advice, but not cross-domain method claims, systematic-review reporting requirements, or publication rights beyond the captured instructions.
