## Operational rules

- Route here for taxonomy-design questions: defining a meta-characteristic, selecting dimensions and characteristics, alternating empirical and conceptual passes, and setting ending conditions.
- Pair this card with `kundisch_taxonomy_update_2021` when the user needs post-2013 guidance on taxonomy evaluation, taxonomy operations, reporting transparency, or iterative update/evolution.
- For survey-writing use, label any transfer from IS taxonomy development to NLP/CS survey taxonomy design as local adaptation unless the target claim is directly about taxonomy development.
- Do not use this source alone to answer formal SR search, screening, eligibility, risk-of-bias, certainty, or PRISMA-style reporting questions.

## Common misuses

- Treating the method as evidence for how to run a literature search or screen papers.
- Treating the five useful-taxonomy attributes as a universal rubric for review quality.
- Claiming the method produces the single best taxonomy for a domain.
- Ignoring researcher judgment, meta-characteristic choice, or ending-condition subjectivity.
