## Operational rules

- Use JBI when the review question is broader than Cochrane intervention-effect reviews, especially scoping, qualitative, textual, mixed-methods, umbrella, or policy-related evidence syntheses.
- Prefer the online manual over the PDF for freshness-sensitive claims, and treat the PDF as a versioned local reference.
- Route PRISMA reporting, GRADE certainty, and Cochrane publication-standard claims to their dedicated source IDs.
- For prevalence/incidence, diagnostic test accuracy, and measurement-property reviews, verify the external methodological guidance named by JBI.

## Common misuses

- Treating the 2024 PDF as the final current authority when the online manual may have newer revisions.
- Using JBI as a PRISMA reporting guideline or GRADE certainty framework.
- Applying JBI healthcare evidence-synthesis methods to general CS/NLP literature surveys without labeling the adaptation.

## Evidence limits

This card relies on the online overview and local PDF conversion for high-level scope and boundary claims. The PDF section index is page-based, so exact chapter-level claims need targeted searches or the online chapter source before final use.
