## Operational rules

- Route here for practical narrative biomedical review writing advice, especially manuscript structure, search-method transparency, source organization, and common writing problems.
- Treat the duplicate `extra_1` Markdown as the same captured PDF content, not a separate independent source.
- Pair this source with formal SR or reporting sources when the task involves systematic reviews, PRISMA, SWiM, Cochrane, or checklist compliance.
- Label any transfer from biomedical narrative reviews to broader AI/CS survey writing as an adaptation.

## Common misuses

- Treating this paper as a formal reporting guideline.
- Using 2011 publication-process advice as current journal policy without verification.
- Applying biomedical review-writing norms directly to non-biomedical survey genres.
- Letting the article's practical advice override source-specific reporting standards.
