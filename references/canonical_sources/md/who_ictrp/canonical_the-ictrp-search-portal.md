# ICTRP Search Portal

The [ICTRP Search Portal](https://trialsearch.who.int/Default.aspx) aims to provide a single point of access to information about ongoing and completed clinical trials. It provides a searchable database containing the trial registration data sets made available by data providers around the world meeting criteria for content and quality control.

Trial registration data sets are available on the ICTRP Search Portal in English only. Some data providers, however, may also store trial registration data sets in other languages. If available, these may be searched by going directly to the web sites of the individual registries. It is possible to search for trials registered in certain Primary Registries in the following languages:

- [Chinese](https://www.chictr.org.cn/) [- Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR)](https://www.chictr.org.cn/)

- [Dutch - The Netherlands Trial Register (CCMO)](http://www.onderzoekmetmensen.nl/)

- [German](https://www.drks.de/) [– German Clinical Trials Register (DRKS)](https://www.drks.de/)

- [Japanese](https://rctportal.niph.go.jp/en/) [– Japan Primary Registries Network (JPRN)](https://rctportal.niph.go.jp/en/)

- [Korean](http://cris.nih.go.kr/cris/index.jsp) [– Clinical Research Information Service (CRiS), Republic of Korea](http://cris.nih.go.kr/cris/index.jsp)

- [Persian](http://www.irct.ir/) [– Iranian Registry of Clinical Trials (IRCT)](http://www.irct.ir/)

- [Peruvian](https://ensayosclinicos-repec.ins.gob.pe/en/) [Clinical Trial Registry (REPEC)](https://ensayosclinicos-repec.ins.gob.pe/en/)

- [Portuguese](http://www.ensaiosclinicos.gov.br/) [– Brazilian Registry of Clinical Trials (ReBec)](http://www.ensaiosclinicos.gov.br/)

- [Spanish](https://rpcec.sld.cu/en) [– Cuban Public Registry of Clinical Trials(RPCEC)](https://rpcec.sld.cu/en) and the [Peruvian Clinical Trial Registry (REPEC)](https://ensayosclinicos-repec.ins.gob.pe/)

To facilitate the unambiguous identification of trials, the Search Portal bridges (groups together) multiple records about the same trial.

The ICTRP Search Portal has been developed to make it easier for users to search for clinical trials. Please [contact us](https://trialsearch.who.int/Contact.aspx) if you have any comments or suggestions.
