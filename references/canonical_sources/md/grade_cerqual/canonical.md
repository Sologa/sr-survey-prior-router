# Confidence in the Evidence from Reviews of Qualitative Research

### What is the GRADE-CERQual approach?

The GRADE-CERQual approach provides a transparent method for assessing the confidence of evidence from reviews of qualitative research, and indicating this confidence to end users, such as guideline panels or decision makers. CERQual uses a similar approach conceptually to other GRADE tools, but is intended for findings from systematic reviews of qualitative evidence.

### [Have you tried our online tool?](https://isoq.epistemonikos.org/)

[The](https://isoq.epistemonikos.org/) [interactive Summary of Qualitative Findings (iSoQ) tool](http://isoq.epistemonikos.org) is a free online platform designed to help review authors apply the GRADE-CERQual approach to the findings of their qualitative evidence synthesis (QES).

### [CAMELOT Tool for Assessing Methodological Limitations](https://www.cerqual.org/methodological-limitations-component/)

[Assessing the](https://www.cerqual.org/methodological-limitations-component/) [methodological limitations](https://www.cerqual.org/methodological-limitations-component/) of the evidence contributing to a review finding is one component of the GRADE-CERQual approach. The [CAMELOT tool](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/cesm.12058) for assessing the methodological limitations of primary studies is compatible with GRADE-CERQual
