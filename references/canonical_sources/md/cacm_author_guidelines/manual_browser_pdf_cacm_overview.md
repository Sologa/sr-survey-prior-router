Overview for CACM
The following resources provide existing and prospective authors with the
information they need to define, compose, and submit an article or manuscript
for Communications of the ACM, the flagship publication of the ACM.
Author Guidelines and Submission Information
Authors are strongly encouraged to start with the Author Guidelines  first to
better understand the scope of submissions and the competition for their
acceptance. Specific submission information is included here as it depends on the
section of CACM that an author wishes to submit to.
Information Regarding Policy, Rights, and Permissions
Review ACM's Publishing Policy  for information covering the transfer and
publishing license agreements and permissions.
Review ACM's Author Rights  for information regarding authors' rights
management and Open Access options.
Promotion Information
Kudos is a web-based service that helps CACM authors increase the visibility and
impact of their articles. ACM makes the service available to CACM contact
authors who want assistance with increasing usage of and citation to their
articles. Authors can view the information regarding the Kudos service here .
Follow us on X (Twitter) .
Contact Us
Authors with feedback or questions about submissions not answered by
the Author Guidelines  are encouraged to Contact Us .
