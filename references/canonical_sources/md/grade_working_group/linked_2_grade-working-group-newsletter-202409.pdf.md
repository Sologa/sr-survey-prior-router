1. Summary from the GRADE Guidance Group (G3)
Message from the G3: Welcome to the first edition of the GRADE Working Group
newsletter! In this issue, we bring you the latest updates, exciting news, and important
information from across our Working Group. Our commitment to advancing the quality
of healthcare guidelines, systematic reviews and health decision products through
rigorous and transparent methods remains steadfast. We are thrilled to share our
progress and look forward to your continued support and engagement. Section 2
contains news from centers and networks, section 3 news from project groups,
section 4 on the new GRADE Book, section 5 information on future meetings and
section 6 is for photos. We will use this template for future newsletters that we plan to
publish periodically and if you did not make it for this deadline, we will include your
information in the next edition.
Follow us on LinkedIn for more updates and join the conversation with our growing
vibrant community: https://www.linkedin.com/company/grade-working-group/ !

4. Update from the GRADE Book Team
Exciting Developments of the one stop shop go to source for GRADE guidance
for guideline development and implementation organizations, end-users of
recommendations (e.g., health workers), and hard-core methodologists (e.g.,
those supporting guideline developers or systematic review authors):
• The new GRADE Book is an official publication of the GRADE working group,
providing the most up-to-date and accurate description of the GRADE approach.
It incorporates the latest advancements, methodologies, and best practices,
ensuring that users have access to practical guidance for assessing the certainty
of evidence and formulating recommendations. Access the book here:
https://book.gradepro.org/
By the end of 2026, the GRADE Book will fully replace the GRADE Handbook,
which originated in 2003 and was last fully updated in 2013. As of September
2024, only select chapters dealing with rating the certainty in intervention effects
have been written and made available, with new content being added regularly
as the GRADE working group, through its GRADE Guidance Group (G3),
continues its review and production process. For sections that have not yet been
updated, please refer to the original GRADE Handbook.
• GRADE GPT: The GRADE Book IA Companion
In parallel with the GRADE Book, users can access the GRADE framework
through the GRADE GPT app, a cutting-edge, AI-powered companion. Trained
specifically on the GRADE Book content and other official GRADE products, this
app offers an interactive, intuitive way to engage with GRADE material. Though
still in beta, GRADE GPT promises instant access to approved, official guidance
and is continually being refined. Users can explore the app and provide feedback
on its development here: The GRADE Book IA Companion will ONLY use
approved content, that is officially sanctioned GRADE guidance, concepts and
material. You can test and use the companion here: https://chatgpt.com/g/g-
F1ZjwVYOk-grade-gpt
