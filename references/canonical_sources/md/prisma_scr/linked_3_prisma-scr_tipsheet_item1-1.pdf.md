## Page 1

Tips for reporting this item: 
 
Include the term “scoping review” in the title of the review 
 
Ensure that the title reflects the key components that 
inform the eligibility criteria of the scoping review (i.e., 
population, concept, context) 
 
Example:  
“Screening a cognitive impairment in the dialysis population: a 
scoping review” 
 
San A, Hiremagalur B, Muircroft W, Grealish L. Screening of cognitive impairment in the dialysis 
population: a scoping review. Dement Geriatr Cogn Disord. 2017;44:182-95. [PMID: 28869959] 
doi:10.1159/000479679
