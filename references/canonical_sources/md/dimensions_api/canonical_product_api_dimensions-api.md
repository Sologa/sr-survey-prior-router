The Dimensions API provides full-text search and data retrieval for use in [complex analyses and visualizations](https://www.dimensions.ai/products/all-products/type/dashboard) . The API uses an intuitive query language specifically developed for Dimensions data. You can retrieve, aggregate, and sort data from highly specific requests in a single API call. Additional services like text categorization are also available , allowing you to efficiently classify and interpret large datasets based on your unique requirements.

## What Dimensions API can do for you

- Competitive intelligence: Our analytics API allows you to stay ahead of the curve by helping you analyze competitor strategies and performance metrics.

- Identifying emerging research activity: Track rising publication volumes, funding shifts, researcher contributions and more to spot new research trends.

- Innovation landscape mapping: Visualize the interplay between various research outputs and their impact on innovation within your field.

- [Academic and industry partnerships](https://www.dimensions.ai/products/all-products/dimensions-industry-partnerships) and collaboration networks: Foster relationships and collaboration opportunities by mapping institutional connections and partnerships.

- Key Opinion Leader (KOL) identification: Pinpoint influential figures in your field to enhance your outreach and strategic alliances.

- Recruitment and talent: Streamline your recruitment processes by identifying and connecting with top talent in your industry.

- Performance and benchmarking: Use our data integration and aggregation tool to evaluate your organization’s performance against industry standards and competitors. This process will help you identify areas for improvement.

- Tracking funding dollar flows and citation patterns: Gain insights into funding trends and their correlation with citation metrics, aiding in financial planning and resource allocation.

- Literature gap analysis: Discover uncharted areas within your field to effectively direct your research efforts there.

- Marketing and communication strategy: Refine your messaging and outreach efforts based on comprehensive data analysis.

- [Social and economic impact of research](https://www.dimensions.ai/sector/dimensions-for-government) : Understand and communicate the broader implications of your research in real-world contexts.
