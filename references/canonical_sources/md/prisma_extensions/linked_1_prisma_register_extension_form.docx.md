Registering a PRISMA extension

Several extensions to PRISMA have been developed to cover aspects of reporting not captured in the main 2020 Statement. These extensions provide reporting guidance for reviews that, for example, address particular review questions (e.g. diagnostic test accuracy), or use particular data sources (e.g. individual participant data). Additional extensions that address aspects not currently covered might be developed.

If you are interested in developing a new PRISMA 2020 extension, please complete the application below and email it to prismastatement@gmail.com. The PRISMA Executive* will consider your proposal against the criteria available here at our next meeting (which occur every two months).

1. Proposed title of the PRISMA 2020 extension

2. Name, institution and country of the extension lead(s)

3. Email address(es) of extension lead(s)

4. Members of the core extension team, their institutions, and countries

5. Outline the core extension team’s experience and expertise in:

reporting guideline development (or other research projects using similar methodology) (e.g., names of guidelines to which the team have contributed as authors); and,

content expertise relevant to the proposed extension.

6. Outline any plans for patient and public involvement in the development of the extension:

7. Provide a justification for why an extension is required. This is likely to include:

an explanation of what items / elements are currently missing from PRISMA 2020 and its current extensions, including why those elements are important;

an assessment of reporting quality of items potentially relevant for the proposed extension; and,

the number of reviews that would be affected by the new extension, such as the prevalence of similar reviews in recent years or evidence that the review type is likely to become prevalent.

For i), please indicate on the PRISMA 2020 expanded checklist (available at the end of this document) where there are missing items / elements.

8. Do you plan to develop a PRISMA statement for protocols concurrently with the extension for reporting the review?

9. List other PRISMA extensions with related content and how this extension is similar or different. Describe how developers of related extensions will be involved in the development of the new extension.

10. Outline the methods you will use to develop the guideline. If you have a protocol, you can submit the protocol with this application.

11. Please indicate what funding and/or other commitments you have to support the development of the reporting guideline (e.g. funding for a consensus meeting, institutional salary support)

12. The PRISMA Executive* would like PRISMA extensions to be published under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0) license and to be open access (i.e. not behind a paywall). The Creative Commons license allows the extension to be translated, and for the items and elements to be used in subsidiary research. Please note that in making the extension open access, you may incur an article processing fee.

Are you prepared to publish the extension with a CC BY-SA 4.0 license and as an open access article?

13. The PRISMA Executive* and the PRISMATIC team** are leading initiatives to improve the usability and uptake of PRISMA and its extensions, including:

developing a unified PRISMA statement; a statement that includes reporting items and elements from PRISMA 2020 and the suite of extensions, and,

organising an annual PRISMA Symposium; this virtual event aims to provide information to developers, facilitate discussion across extension teams, develop closer working relationships and work towards greater harmonisation across the extensions.

Would you be prepared to engage with these initiatives?
