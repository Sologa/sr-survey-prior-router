# PRISMA 2020 statement paper

The PRISMA 2020 [statement paper](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.n71) includes a description of how the reporting guideline was developed, and presents the 27-item checklist , an expanded checklist that details reporting recommendations for each item, the PRISMA 2020 abstract checklist , and template flow diagrams for original and updated reviews. Users are advised to read the statement paper in conjunction with the PRISMA 2020 explanation and elaboration paper .

In order to encourage dissemination of the PRISMA 2020 statement paper, it has been published in several journals:

-
Page MJ, McKenzie JE, Bossuyt PM, Boutron I, Hoffmann TC, Mulrow CD, et al. The PRISMA 2020 statement: an updated guideline for reporting systematic reviews. BMJ 2021;372:n71. doi: [10.1136/bmj.n71](http://dx.doi.org/10.1136/bmj.n71)

-
