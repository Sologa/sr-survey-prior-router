# PRISMA 2020 for Abstracts

The PRISMA 2020 extension for Abstracts was published within the main [PRISMA 2020 paper](http://dx.doi.org/10.1136/bmj.n71) . The 12-item checklist gives authors a framework for condensing their systematic review into the essentials for a journal or conference abstract that will meet the needs of many readers.

Note: This abstract checklist is an update of the [2013 PRISMA for Abstracts statement](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1001419) , reflecting new and modified content in PRISMA 2020.
