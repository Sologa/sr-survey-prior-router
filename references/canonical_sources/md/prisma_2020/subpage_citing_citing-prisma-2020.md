# Citing PRISMA 2020

When referring to the PRISMA 2020 in reports and presentations, we recommend citing one of the journal articles presenting the [statement](http://dx.doi.org/10.1136/bmj.n71) and the [explanation and elaboration](http://dx.doi.org/10.1136/bmj.n160) , rather than referring to the PRISMA website.
