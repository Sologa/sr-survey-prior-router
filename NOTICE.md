The local Markdown corpus is provided as a review and routing aid. It may include conversion artifacts and stale captures. Treat it as a local evidence locator, not as a replacement for the original source or a final rights decision.

Manual browser captures, especially `press` and `cacm_author_guidelines`, were added to overcome scripted access blocks. Those captures remain refresh-sensitive and rights-sensitive. Verify current publisher/source pages and reuse terms before public redistribution or substantial quotation.
